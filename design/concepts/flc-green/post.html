Novices ran the show at Autocross #4

Lead photo — 3:2
Author
Dan Reeves June 14, 2026 · 6 min read

Forty-one cars turned out to the Osceola lot on a morning that stayed under ninety degrees, which around here counts as a gift. Nineteen of them belonged to drivers running their first event, and by the third heat the gap between the novice group and the regulars had closed to under a second.

The course

A long sweeper into a tight slalom, then a chicago box that punished anyone carrying speed into the entry. Cone count stayed low, which says more about the course design than the driving.

If you can hold a clean line through the box, you can hold one anywhere in the state.

Results and what's next

Timing sheets are posted on ClubRegistration, and the season standings update Wednesday. Autocross #5 opens for registration two weeks out — the novice school runs the Friday evening before.
